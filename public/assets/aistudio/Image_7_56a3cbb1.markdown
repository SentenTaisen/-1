## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of artisanal third-wave coffee ritual and high-velocity digital fulfillment. It communicates uncompromising craft, speed, precision, and nocturnal luxury. Designed for urban professionals, coffee purists, and design-conscious epicures, the interface establishes an atmosphere of seamless, bespoke service.

The design movement synthesizes **Modern Dark Glassmorphism** with **Neo-Tactile Pill Architecture**. The foundational aesthetic sits on deep velvety slate and smoked obsidian substrates, elevated by hyper-refined micro-borders, atmospheric ambient color glows, and vivid, saturated jewel-tone pills. Rather than clinical minimalism, this system exudes sensory warmth through tactile card surfaces, espresso-tinted luminescence, and snappy, micro-curated navigational hubs.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for visual hierarchy and appetite appeal against a deeply saturated, glare-free dark substrate:

- **Primary (`#FF9F43` - Crema Amber):** The heart of the brand. Evokes golden-hour pour-overs, rich crema, and swift kinetic energy. Used for primary CTAs, critical order timers, and focal status highlights.
- **Secondary (`#00F2FE` - Electric Nitrogen Cyan):** Evokes cold brew extraction, cryogenic flash-chilling, and ultra-fast rapid-courier transit markers. Used for active tracking states, telemetry, and live dispatch cues.
- **Tertiary (`#A78BFA` - Lavender Bloom / Foam Lilac):** A soft, sophisticated lilac that balances the heat of the amber with modern editorial elegance. Used for subscription badges, specialty single-origin highlights, and tertiary interactive states.
- **Neutral (`#0F1117` - Obsidian Roast):** An ultra-deep carbon neutral base that prevents pure-black eye fatigue while lending high contrast to glass layers (`#181B24`, `#202533`, and `#2B3245`).

### Functional Accents
- **Warm Caramel / Toast (`#FBBF24`):** Roast profile tags, acidity meters, and user rating indicators.
- **Dragonfruit Punch (`#F43F5E`):** Flash drop alerts, canceled orders, and urgent notifications.
- **Matcha Glow (`#10B981`):** Ready-for-pickup badges, courier arrival verification, and success states.

## Typography

The type system blends the technical, forward-leaning cadence of **Space Grotesk** with the fluid, ultra-legible humanist geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Space Grotesk (Display & Headlines):** Infuses brand moments with industrial craft, calibrated roasting metrics, extraction temperatures, and punchy titles. Tight negative tracking creates high-density impact.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Micro-UI):** Delivers effortless scannability in dimly lit mobile environments. Its generous apertures prevent dense bean tasting notes or order breakdowns from clustering together visually.
- **Upper-case Tag Utility (`label-tag`):** Applied exclusively to roast categories (`LIGHT ETHIOPIAN`, `FLASH CHILL`, `AEROPRESS`), order status timestamps, and metadata chips.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an adaptable 12-column responsive fluid grid designed around dedicated app trays, cockpit dashboards, and checkout rails:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid featuring a fixed floating sidebar navigation (280px), a fluid central ordering / discovery canvas, and an optional collapsible floating drawer for current brew status / cart drawer (380px). Gutters set to `1.75rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Sidebar collapses into an expansive icon rail or elevated bottom nav pill. Main content stretches with `2rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with fixed edge margins of `1rem`. Interfaces prioritize full-bleed vertical scrolling cards, sticky action trays, and thumb-accessible horizontal pill-scroll rails.

### Spacing Rhythm
Built strictly on a foundational 4px/8px incremental rhythm. Internal padding within cards relies on `space-md` (`1rem`) to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`), keeping content compact and structured without sacrificing airiness.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on harsh drop shadows; instead, it utilizes **tonal surface stacking**, **translucent glass backdrops**, and **subtle ambient glow perimeters**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Ground substrate at `#0B0D13`. Solid, non-distracting void.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Modular Modules):** `#141721` with a delicate, continuous 1px ghost border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`). Provides structural boundaries without heaviness.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Trays & Modals):** `#1B202E` at 85% opacity with an active `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`. Border highlights increase slightly to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Layer 3 (Active Interactivity / Popovers):** Elevated with an ambient directional rim glow tinted by context:
  - Default: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`
  - Amber Pulse (Active Order / Checkout): `0 10px 28px -6px rgba(255, 159, 67, 0.25)`
  - Electric Dispatch Pulse: `0 10px 28px -6px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.22)`

## Shapes

The design system champions soft, organic geometry with high-radii corners that echo modern automotive dashboards and ergonomic consumer hardware:

- **Containers & Big Cards:** Feature `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) corners, giving prominent modules a pebble-like tactility.
- **Buttons, Selection Chips & Inputs:** Adopt fully rounded **pills** (`9999px`) or `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) depending on contextual density. Buttons hosting high-urgency actions ("Brew Now", "Order Ahead") use fluid pill contours.
- **Image Frames & Product Thumbs:** Standardized at `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) with an inner `1px` subtle rim to prevent photographic blends into the dark background.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Crema Button):** High-contrast filled pill in `#FF9F43` with `#0B0D13` bold text. Includes subtle top inner reflection (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.4)`). Active tap scales down to `0.98`.
- **Secondary (Neon Nitro Action):** Translucent cyan tint (`rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.12)`) with a crisp `#00F2FE` border and electric text. Used for tracking, route view, and custom brew toggles.
- **Ghost / Glass Button:** Dark translucent fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`), text `#E2E8F0`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Hover transitions to 12% fill opacity.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Floating horizontal scrollers. Inactive chips feature dark charcoal backgrounds (`#181B24`) with muted slate text (`#94A3B8`). Active chips illuminate with vibrant contrast: filled Lilac (`#A78BFA`), Amber (`#FF9F43`), or Mint (`#10B981`) paired with stark black or high-contrast white text.

### Coffee Menu & Status Cards
- Structured dark containers featuring layered hierarchy: high-resolution transparent product photography, bold origin typography, roast intensity bars (rendered as micro segmented lines), and an instant-action "+" pill docked at the bottom right.
- **Specialty Variant Cards:** Highlight cards with saturated full-color gradient backgrounds (soft amber-to-rose or deep purple glass) for limited micro-lot beans.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Recessed inset containers (`#0E1118`) with generous horizontal padding, integrated search icon, clear action pills, and subtle keyboard shortcut tags (`⌘K`). Focus state activates a 1px halo in `#00F2FE` with 15% atmospheric spread.

### Checkboxes, Steppers & Radios
- **Roast & Grind Radios:** Segmented pill toggle controls with animated sliding capsules that move smoothly between "Whole Bean", "Coarse / French Press", and "Espresso Fine".
- **Milk & Sweetener Checkboxes:** Custom rounded-sm tiles with an animated inner glowing dot upon selection rather than default platform checks.

### Delivery Telemetry Tracker
- Multi-tier timeline card displaying real-time extraction metrics: "Grinding & Dosing", "Hydro-Pressure Extraction", and "Rapid Courier Transit". Visualized using an energetic animated neon cyan progress thread terminating in an illuminated pulse ring.
